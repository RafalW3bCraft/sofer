# sofer demo — shot list

Target length: **150–165 seconds** of actual recording (the form's cap is 3
minutes; this leaves headroom for an intro card and a closing card).

You can record on screen, on a phone using a screen mirror to the browser at
`http://127.0.0.1:5173`, or by pointing a camera at the running app. Keep the
window clean and free of notifications, terminal output, and unrelated tabs.

## Before you record

1. `npm install && npm --prefix client install && npm --prefix server install`
2. `npm run dev` — Vite at `:5173`, Express at `:3001`.
3. Open `http://127.0.0.1:5173/` in a clean window at desktop width (1280×800)
   or mobile width (390×844). Pick one and stay with it.
4. (Optional) Open DevTools → Application → Local Storage and clear it, so the
   recording shows a fresh learner.

## The 150–165 second shot list

| # | Time | What to show | What to do |
|---|------|--------------|------------|
| 1 | 0:00–0:08 | The problem | A short spoken line: "Most beginner Arabic apps explain the letters but skip the sentences. sofer starts with real sentences you can tap." |
| 2 | 0:08–0:15 | Home screen | Show the sofer homepage. Click **Start Learning**. |
| 3 | 0:15–0:30 | Lesson list | The three lessons (Greetings, Numbers, Directions). Open **Greetings**. |
| 4 | 0:30–0:55 | First sentence + tap a token | The greeting ٱلسَّلَامُ عَلَيْكُمْ renders in RTL. Click the word ٱلسَّلَامُ. The TokenPanel opens — show the contextual meaning, part of speech, grammar note, and Listen button. |
| 5 | 0:55–1:10 | Keyboard navigation | Press Escape to close. Tab to focus a token. Arrow Left / Arrow Right to step through tokens. Enter to open the panel again. |
| 6 | 1:10–1:25 | Mark as tricky | In the TokenPanel, click **Mark as tricky**. The button label flips to **Marked tricky**. |
| 7 | 1:25–1:45 | Comprehension check | Scroll to the question. Pick the correct option. Click **Check answer**. The curated explanation appears. Click **Next sentence**. |
| 8 | 1:45–2:00 | Progress + reload persistence | Click the **Progress** link. Show "Lessons completed: 1", "Questions answered: 1", "Accuracy: 100%". **Reload the tab.** Return to Progress. The counts are still there. |
| 9 | 2:00–2:15 | Tricky token | Show the **Tricky tokens** section in Progress listing the word you marked. Click **Jump to lesson** to verify it routes back. |
| 10 | 2:15–2:30 | Alphabet | Open **Alphabet**. Show the 28-letter grid, scroll to diacritics. |
| 11 | 2:30–2:40 | AI tutor (optional) | On any token, click **Explain with AI tutor**. If a real `TOKENFACTORY_API_KEY` is in `.env`, the response labels itself "Generated by AI · Model X". If not, the "AI tutor unavailable" pill appears with the curated fallback. Either is honest. |
| 12 | 2:40–2:45 | Recap | One spoken sentence: "A real Arabic sentence, a tap that teaches, progress that persists. That's sofer." |

## What NOT to do

- Don't narrate the code, the framework, or the AI provider's docs.
- Don't show credentials, the `.env` file, or the server terminal.
- Don't switch tabs, take a phone call, or open notifications.
- Don't use the simulator's slow-mode; record at native speed.
